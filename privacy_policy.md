# WeatherDex Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 25, 2026**
**Developer: Elias Forge**

---

## Overview

WeatherDex is a weather application built to give you accurate, timely, and intelligent weather information. We believe privacy is a right, not a feature. This policy explains exactly what data WeatherDex collects, why, and what we do with it.

The short version: **your data stays on your device. We don't sell it, share it, or profit from it.**

---

## Data We Collect

### Location
WeatherDex requests access to your device's location to fetch weather conditions, forecasts, and alerts for your area. Location is used locally on your device to make API requests to weather data providers. We do not store, transmit, or retain your location data on any Elias Forge server.

### Nothing Else
WeatherDex does not collect:
- Your name, email, or any account information
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Crash reports sent to our servers
- Any data for advertising purposes

---

## How Your Location Is Used

When you open WeatherDex or the app refreshes in the background, your device's coordinates are used to query the following third-party weather data providers:

| Provider | What we request | Their privacy policy |
|----------|----------------|----------------------|
| National Weather Service (NWS/NOAA) | Forecasts, observations, alerts | weather.gov |
| WAQI (World Air Quality Index) | Air quality index | waqi.info/privacy |
| Open-Meteo | UV index | open-meteo.com/privacy |
| Sunrise-Sunset.org | Sunrise and sunset times | sunrise-sunset.org |

These requests are made directly from your device. Each provider receives your approximate coordinates to return local weather data. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

---

## Background Location

WeatherDex may request background location access to refresh weather data and deliver timely alerts when the app is not open. This is used solely to keep your weather information current and to power WatchTower alert notifications. Background location is never used for tracking, advertising, or any purpose beyond weather data retrieval.

---

## WatchTower & Notifications

WeatherDex includes WatchTower, an alert intelligence system that monitors NWS weather alerts and delivers notifications based on severity. These notifications are generated entirely on your device using data from NWS. No personal data is transmitted to Elias Forge in connection with this feature.

---

## Data Storage

All weather data fetched by WeatherDex is stored locally on your device in app-specific storage. This data is not accessible to other apps and is deleted when you uninstall WeatherDex. We do not operate any cloud storage or databases containing user data.

---

## Third-Party Services

WeatherDex does not integrate any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or social media tracking. The only third-party connections WeatherDex makes are to the weather data providers listed above, solely to retrieve weather information.

---

## Children's Privacy

WeatherDex does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no features directed at children and no mechanisms for collecting personal information.
